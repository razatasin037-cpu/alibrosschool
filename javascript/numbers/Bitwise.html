Bitwise Operators Kya Hain?

JavaScript me Bitwise Operators numbers ko binary bits ke level par compare aur modify karte hain.

Normal arithmetic operators numbers par calculation karte hain, jabki Bitwise Operators number ko binary form me process karte hain. Jaise &, |, ^, ~, <<, >> aur >>>.

Bitwise Operators List

Operator Name Use
& AND Dono bits 1 hon to result 1.
| OR Koi ek bit 1 ho to result 1.
^ XOR Bits different hon to result 1.
~ NOT Bits ko reverse karta hai.
<< Left Shift Bits ko left side shift karta hai.
>> Signed Right Shift Bits ko right shift karta hai.
>>> Unsigned Right Shift Zero-fill ke saath right shift karta hai.

Simple Binary Example

Number 5 ka binary 0101 hota hai aur 3 ka binary 0011 hota hai.

Number Binary
5 0101
3 0011

Bitwise AND (&)

Dono corresponding bits 1 hon tab result 1 hota hai. 

let result = 5 & 3;

console.log(result);

Output:

1
0101
0011
----
0001 = 1

Bitwise OR (|)

Agar corresponding bits me se koi ek bhi 1 hai to result 1 hota hai. 

let result = 5 | 3;

console.log(result);

Output:

7
0101
0011
----
0111 = 7

Bitwise XOR (^)

Agar dono bits different hain to result 1 hota hai. 

let result = 5 ^ 3;

console.log(result);

Output:

6

Bitwise NOT (~)

~ bits ko reverse karta hai. 

let result = ~5;

console.log(result);

Output:

-6

Easy rule: ~x = -(x + 1).

Left Shift (<<)

Bits ko left side shift karta hai. 

let result = 5 << 1;

console.log(result);

Output:

10

Ek position left shift karna is case me value ko 2 se multiply karne jaisa result deta hai: 5 × 2 = 10.

Signed Right Shift (>>)

Bits ko right side shift karta hai aur sign ko preserve karta hai. 

let result = 10 >> 1;

console.log(result);

Output:

5

Unsigned Right Shift (>>>)

Bits ko right shift karta hai aur left side me zero fill karta hai. 

let result = 10 >>> 1;

console.log(result);

Output:

5

Quick Bitwise Reference

Expression Result
5 & 3 1
5 | 3 7
5 ^ 3 6
~5 -6
5 << 1 10
10 >> 1 5
10 >>> 1 5

Important Points

1. Bitwise operators numbers ke binary bits par kaam karte hain.

2. & AND operator hai.

3. | OR operator hai.

4. ^ XOR operator hai.

5. ~ NOT operator hai.

6. << left shift aur >> right shift karta hai.

7. JavaScript Number bitwise operations me values ko generally 32-bit integer representation me process karta hai.
